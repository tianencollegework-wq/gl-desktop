1. About the Software

1.1 The Software is the Windows desktop client of the Gracelink instant-messaging
service. It requires an Internet connection to the servers provided by Gracelink and a
Gracelink account in order to sign in.
1.2 The Software contains the client only; it includes no server-side software. The
servers, the account system and the related services are operated and maintained by the
Gracelink Team ("we", "us").
1.3 Main features: one-to-one and group chat (text, emoji, images, files and voice
messages, with quoting, forwarding and recall), contacts and friend management, group
management (members and admins, group announcement, group mute), one-to-one and
multi-party group audio/video calls (including screen sharing), signing in by scanning a
QR code, multiple accounts and a multi-language interface, and the content services in
the "Discover" section (Media news and Lingting music).
